A few helpful notes

Good to know.

Small details, clearly explained.

What kinds of trips does Curaters offer?+

Day trips, group trips, private trips and hosted trips. The existing offers and their supplied prices appear above.

What happens when I prepare a booking email?+

Your device’s email app should open with a draft addressed to travelcuraters@gmail.com. Review it and send it yourself; this website does not submit, store or confirm a booking.

Can I ask about a destination that is not listed?+

Use the email link to contact Curaters directly at travelcuraters@gmail.com.

Does the private access field check my key?+

No. The supplied preview contains no key-verification service. A key typed in the field remains on your device and is cleared when you continue to the private-trip enquiry.
